reIDM Integration

Hands downloads and page media to the reIDM download manager. Independent of Tonec's IDM extension.

Connection

Capture

Take over browser downloads
When the browser starts saving a file, cancel it and hand the URL to reIDM instead. Very small files may still finish before the extension can cancel them — that is a browser limitation, not a setting.
Look for video and audio
Watches network requests for streams and media files (HLS/DASH manifests, mp4, webm, audio) and lists them in the popup. Needs broad host permissions, which is why the extension asks for them.

Never capture

Requests to reIDM are made from the extension's service worker, so they carry a chrome-extension:// origin. reIDM accepts only extension origins and local processes on that endpoint, which is what keeps ordinary web pages out.
